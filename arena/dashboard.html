Rows are brains (combat strategies; "fastswing" is the current best and the reference, others are named by what they add to it). The Eval is one wave schedule, selected above. Italic rows with ~ were never run on this eval: their numbers are estimated from the eval where they were run, as that run's difference from fastswing, shifted onto this eval's fastswing level, so the table is always filled in. Score is the headline number (higher is better; 0 = the average baseline run in that eval, +1 = one standard deviation better). p-values are one-sided on the Score: is this brain better than the chosen comparison brain (Welch t-test, Mann-Whitney U, paired on wave seed). green p<0.05, amber p<0.15. Half-width is the 80% confidence interval of the mean Score.
What the Score is made of
One number per run, built from four measures of how well the bot fought. Each is log-transformed and standardised against that eval's baseline runs, then combined with fixed weights (the first principal component, i.e. the shared "fighting well" axis), frozen so the Score never drifts. A minor creeper penalty is then subtracted.
Creeper penalty: −0.05 per HP of damage the bot actually took from creeper blasts (max −1 at 20 HP). A blast the shield absorbed, or that only hurt other mobs, costs nothing, and a clean creeper kill costs nothing. Score = Σ weight × standardised measure − 0.05 × min(20, creeper blast damage taken). On small improvements the composite has ~35–40% less relative noise than survival time alone.
